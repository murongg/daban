# Sidebar search and organization

[User guide](README.md) · [简体中文](../zh-CN/organization.md)

Use the sidebar to switch Bots, groups, and main pages. List organization and group collaboration are different features.

## Search

Choose the Bot-and-conversation search field, or press Cmd + K / Ctrl + K. Search names and conversation keywords. Results depend on content available in the current workspace; no result is not proof that the backend never saved it. Inspect older material through conversation history.

## Pins and unread markers

Open a row's organization menu to pin, unpin, or mark it read or unread. These markers do not start tasks, modify permissions, or certify real task completion.

## Custom sections

1. Create a section and give it a name.
2. Use the Bot's organization menu to move it to that section.
3. Rename, reorder, or remove it from a section as needed.

A section is a list category, not a Bot group. Putting Bots together does not share their private chats or dispatch work. Removing a category does not archive its Bots.

## Copy and archive entries

Bot menus provide [Copy Bot](copy-bot.md); group menus can copy an empty group or open archiving. Imports and unconfirmed operations may retain progress entries in the sidebar. Check these before creating another item.

Organization, unread markers, and view preferences belong to this client's workspace rather than a cross-device real-time synchronization service. See [Workspace export](workspace-export.md) for their relationship to retained data.
