# Bot notes

[User guide](README.md) · [简体中文](../zh-CN/notes.md)

Select a Bot and open **Notes** for individual reminders and ideas. These notes belong to that Bot and you.

## Create and follow up

1. Create a note.
2. Enter its title, body, and status, then save.
3. Use search and filters to find it later and change its state to open, later, or completed.
4. You can also explicitly ask the Bot to create, read, or update its notes in conversation.

Example: “Add a note to check the delivery date in our next discussion; do not start any other work.”

Saving a note or marking it completed does not execute a task, notify another Bot, or schedule a run. Create a [schedule](schedules.md) separately for timed execution.

## Drafts and conflicts

Unsaved edits are local drafts. A draft retained after navigation is not evidence of a successful save.

If you and a Bot edit concurrently, compare the original and latest content. Retain your edited fields and review the merge, or adopt the latest version. Preserve newer content in fields you did not change before saving.

An unconfirmed operation can temporarily lock the original submission. Verify its receipt first; retrying the same operation avoids creating another note.

## Trash

Move unwanted notes to trash and restore them later. Archived Bots and environment recovery can make notes read-only; resolve the relevant state before saving.

Notes are neither work preferences nor a workspace-wide task list. Use [Memory](memory.md) to affect future response behavior and [Tasks](tasks.md) for shared follow-up.
